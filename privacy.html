Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Little Athletes Co. (Caio Mathias, sole trader, ABN 60 473 155 778) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we collect the details you enter: centre name, your name, role, suburb, email, phone and your message. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising trackers on this website.

How we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry, arrange a trial session and provide our services. We never sell or rent your information.

How it is sent and stored

Enquiries are delivered to our email inbox through the form service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). Information is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above.

Children's information

We do not collect children's personal information through this website. Information about children in our sessions is handled with the host centre under its policies and with family consent.

Access, correction and complaints

To access, correct or delete your information, or to raise a privacy concern, contact us at littleathletesco@gmail.com or 0473 956 326. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
