Coach Notes: progress you can see

Each term our coach observes every child and records short, individual notes across four areas of development. Your centre receives them at the end of the term — ready for your EYLF documentation and to share with families.

Motor skills

Running, jumping, throwing, catching

Coordination

Balance, body control, spatial awareness

Confidence

Trying new challenges, persistence

Social skills

Taking turns, teamwork, listening

FAQ

Questions centre directors ask us

Straight answers about our childcare sports incursions in Brisbane.

Is the program aligned with the EYLF?

Yes. Sessions are built around fundamental movement skills and support Outcome 3 of the Early Years Learning Framework V2.0 (children have a strong sense of wellbeing, including gross motor development). Coach Notes give your educators documentation they can use in their planning.

Do our educators need to stay during the session?

Yes. Your educators remain with the children to keep ratios and supervision in place, as with any incursion. They do not need to plan, set up or run anything — we bring the session plan and all equipment.

What ages do you work with?

Our Little Movers program is available now for children aged 3 to 6. Programs for school-age children are coming soon.

How does pricing work?

We charge a fixed price per session, billed at the start of each 10-week term. All equipment is included. Contact us for our centre information pack.

Are you Blue Card cleared and insured?

Yes. Our coach holds a valid Queensland Blue Card and current First Aid and CPR certificates, and we carry public liability insurance. Certificates are available on request.

Is the program inclusive of children with autism or additional needs?

Yes. Inclusion is built into every session, with adapted activities, clear routines and visual cues so every child can take part.

Do you run incursions for OSHC and vacation care?

We are taking enquiries from OSHC and vacation care services for 2027. Get in touch to register your interest.

Which areas of Brisbane do you cover?

We visit childcare centres and kindys across Brisbane — north, south, east and west. Ask us about availability in your suburb.
