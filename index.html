FAQ

Questions centre directors ask

Do our educators need to stay during the session?

Yes. Your educators stay with the children to keep ratios and supervision in place, as with any incursion. They don't plan, set up or run anything: we bring the session plan and all equipment.

How is it priced?

A fixed price per session (not per child), invoiced once per term, with all equipment included. Send us an enquiry and we'll send our centre information pack.

What ages and group sizes do you work with?

Little Movers is designed for children aged 3–6. We plan each session around your rooms, numbers and ratios.

Can children with additional needs join in?

Yes. Activities are adapted with clear routines, visual cues and different levels of challenge so every child can take part and succeed.

What do you need from our centre?

A safe indoor or outdoor space, educators present for supervision, and any information about children's additional needs, allergies or medical considerations.

Which Brisbane suburbs do you cover?

We visit childcare centres and kindys across inner, east and south Brisbane, including Brisbane City, East Brisbane, South Brisbane, Kangaroo Point, Woolloongabba, Coorparoo, Greenslopes, Camp Hill, Carina, Holland Park, Annerley, Morningside, Bulimba, Hawthorne, Norman Park, New Farm, Fortitude Valley and Newstead.
